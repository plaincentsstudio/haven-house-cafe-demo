About the café

A café that belongs to its neighbourhood

Haven House Cafe is a community café in North Haven, just east of Nose Hill Park, rated 4.9 stars across 406 reviews. It’s the kind of place where the latte is named after the neighbourhood — the Haven-ahh Latte was created with a North Haven resident — and where regulars, kids, seniors, and dogs are all part of the room.

Licensed, dog-friendly with free treats, and home to an artisan market and live entertainment, it’s a gathering spot first and a café second. The only thing it’s missing is a website — right now, the only way to find it is Instagram or word of mouth.

“The Haven-ahh Latte — created with a North Haven resident.” — Haven House Cafe, North Haven, Calgary
